### 0:25 — Quién es (≈35 s)

[ER] Malenia sentada esperando · [JK] Artemisa de rodillas, lluvia de pétalos, la arena que se oscurece.

### 1:00 — La espada que no para (≈45 s)

[ER] Cadena de cortes y la estocada rápida con el golpe demorado · [JK] lo mismo.

> Malenia's first lesson is simple: she never stops. Her combos keep going after you think they're over.
> In 2D I kept the trap. Three fast cuts… a pause… and a fourth one, late, for the player who rolled too early.

### 1:45 — Se cura cuando te pega (≈40 s)

[ER] la barra de Malenia subiendo después de un golpe · [JK] número rojo de curación sobre Artemisa.

> Every hit she lands, she heals. That's what makes Malenia feel unfair.
> My game has no shield. Your only defense is the parry. So here's my rule: a perfect parry steals nothing back. A hit that gets through feeds her.

### 2:25 — Te castiga si te curás (≈35 s)

[ER] Malenia cruza la arena cuando el jugador toma un frasco · [JK] la estocada de Artemisa contra un jugador curándose.

> Drink a flask in front of Malenia, and she crosses the arena to punish you. Artemisa reads it too. Heal at the wrong distance and she's already on you.

### 3:00 — También se defiende (≈40 s)

[JK] la guardia con escudo, el parry de ella contra el jugador, la patada imparable y el agarre.

> This is where she stops being a copy. My protagonist only parries, so she had to learn that too. Swing at her face and she might block… or parry you back.
> And when she grabs you, it's straight from Elden Ring: lifted on her blade, three hits, and down.

### 3:40 — La Danza del Ave Acuática (≈60 s, el centro del video)

[ER] Waterfowl completo, un solo plano largo · [JK] la danza de Artemisa entera, después en cámara lenta.

> And now… the attack everybody remembers.
> She jumps, she hangs in the air for a second — and she comes down three times, faster than you can read.
> In 2D you can't circle around her. So every burst is a straight line, and the gap between them is your only way out. Jump through the first one, dash through the second, and pray on the third.
